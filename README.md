#  Vrinda Store Annual Sales Dashboard

##  Project Overview

This project presents an **Annual Sales Analysis Dashboard for Vrinda Store (2022)**. The dashboard helps business stakeholders understand customer behavior, sales performance, order trends, and channel-wise contributions to make better data-driven decisions for future growth.

The dashboard is designed to be **interactive, visually intuitive, and business-focused**, suitable for sales review meetings and strategic planning.

---

##  Objectives

* Analyze **annual sales and order trends**
* Understand **customer demographics** (Gender & Age)
* Identify **top-performing states**
* Evaluate **order status performance**
* Compare **sales across online channels**

---

##  Dashboard Features

###  Filters & Slicers

* **Month** – Jan to Dec
* **Channel** – Ajio, Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho
* **Category** – Blouse, Bottom, Ethnic Dress, Kurta

These filters allow dynamic analysis across different business dimensions.

---

###  Key Visuals & Insights

#### 1. Orders vs Sales (Monthly Trend)

* Dual-axis chart showing:

  * **Total Sales Amount**
  * **Total Orders Count**
* Helps track seasonal trends and demand fluctuations

#### 2. Sales: Men vs Women

* Women contribute **~64%** of total sales
* Men contribute **~36%**
* Indicates women as the primary customer segment

#### 3. Order Status Distribution

* **Delivered:** ~92%
* **Cancelled:** ~3%
* **Returned:** ~3%
* **Refunded:** ~2%
* Shows strong delivery success rate

#### 4. Top 5 States by Sales

* Maharashtra
* Karnataka
* Uttar Pradesh
* Telangana
* Tamil Nadu
* Useful for region-focused marketing strategies

#### 5. Orders: Age vs Gender

* Highest orders from **Adult women** segment
* Teenagers and seniors show moderate contribution
* Helps target age-specific promotions

#### 6. Orders by Channel

* **Amazon:** ~35%
* **Myntra:** ~23%
* **Flipkart:** ~22%
* Others: Ajio, Meesho, Nalli
* Identifies the most effective sales platforms
